Windows 98, if it hated you.

unusualityOS 98 is a computer that looks normal on the surface, but the more you explore, the more unusual it becomes. It has  zero patience and a terrible attitude. Features include:

⚬ Lost and Found: Actually loses very important essay and very important presentation the moment you open them.
⚬ MS-DOS Prompt: Makes you say please first, then still tells you command 'please' not found.
⚬ Internet Explorer: Tells you the internet is closed and only open Tuesdays from 2:00 PM to 2:04 PM.
⚬ Minesweeper: Blows up on your first click every single time and roasts you for it.
⚬ Notepad: Swaps positive words like great, good, or happy for bleak replacements like unfortunate, mediocre, or miserable.
⚬ The rest of the desktop includes:
  - a calculator that fudges numbers,
  - a paint canvas that wipes your drawing,
  - an unwinnable Solitaire board, a media player stuck on silence,
  - an address book that blocks everyone you add,
  - a task manager that won't end tasks,
  - a control panel with useless settings,
  - a TI:ME clock that guesses vaguely,
  - window X buttons that dodge your mouse,
  - snarky minimise/maximise popups,
  - a Shut Down menu that won't let you shut down.
